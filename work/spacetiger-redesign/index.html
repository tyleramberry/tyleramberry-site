How we build

Careful by default.
Useful from day one.

AI earns trust by being predictable. Every system we ship runs on the same six rules — written into the build, not left to good intentions.

01

Spec before build

We write down what the system will and won’t do, and agree it with you before any building starts.

02

You approve what matters

A message to a customer or a change to a record can wait for a person to sign off. You decide what runs on its own.

03

It stops rather than guesses

When the answer isn’t in your information, the system flags it instead of making something up.

04

Least access needed

Read-only wherever the job allows, and your data stays in accounts you control.

05

Sensitive files stay out

Files an AI should never read, like credential sheets, are excluded in code — not just by habit.

06

Honest about the options

If an off-the-shelf tool or a better process is the right answer, we say so before you spend on a build.
