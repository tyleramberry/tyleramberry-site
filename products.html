01 Consumer SaaS

Sundae Planner

An AI weekly-planning coach. Talk through your roles, plan actions for each, and it books them on your Google Calendar and checks in daily.

Live in production
sundaeplanner.com Placeholder thumbnail · add img/sundae.jpg

PProblem

Weekly planning works, but nobody sticks with it. Planner apps hold your list and never ask about it, so the plan is dead by Tuesday.

DDecisions

  • Nothing touches your calendar until you approve it. The AI proposes; you confirm.
  • One function decides "done." Every screen shows the same score.
  • Channel-agnostic engines. Web, email, and text share one conversation brain.

OShipped

  • A conversational weekly session that ends in confirmed calendar blocks.
  • A daily cron that follows up by email and reconciles goals against what happened.
  • Onboarding, trials, Stripe billing, analytics. 114 commits from Aug 4, 2026.

03 MSB 341 product · BYU Marriott

My SI

A personal model benchmark: it interviews you, turns your real tasks into tests, and tells you which AI model is best for you.

My SI prototype live
Try My SI

PProblem

Leaderboards test olympiad math, not real work, so nobody knows which model is best for them.

DDecisions

  • Your work, not olympiad math. Test tasks come from an interview about what you actually do.
  • Blind ratings. You judge outputs without knowing which model wrote them.
  • Ship as an MCP connector. It runs inside the AI tools people already use.

OShipped

  • An MCP connector that runs your tasks on GPT, Gemini, and Claude and has you rate them blind.
  • First outside tester: interview to ranking in 15 minutes, for $0.07. Their three-way tie set a rule: ties go to the cheaper, faster model.

TypeScript · Vite · Cloudflare Workers · Supabase · MCP · OpenAI, Gemini & Claude APIs

04 Open-source SaaS

OpenCal

A free, open-source scheduler, designed and shipped for a high-end wedding cake bakery. Clients tap a pickup time and it lands on the owner's Google Calendar, never double-booked.

In progress
github.com/tyleramberry/opencal Placeholder thumbnail · add a booking-flow screenshot

PProblem

Small businesses pay monthly for a booking link that does one thing. The bakery needed one pickup link and a weekly schedule, without a subscription.

DDecisions

  • You host it. One link per business, self-hosted, MIT licensed.
  • Real calendar, race-safe. Calendar busy times block slots live, and a Postgres lock stops two people taking the last slot.
  • Phone required. So the owner can call about an order.

OShipped

  • 30-minute slots, "Add to calendar" confirmations, and self-serve cancel links.
  • Confirmation and cancellation emails with the invite attached.
  • Admin page for hours, address, and pickups.
  • Deploys to Cloudflare Workers or runs locally on PGlite.

05 Open-source plugin · Nous Research hackathon

Hermes Multiplayer

Team chat inside Hermes: channels, DMs, and threads for you, up to four teammates, and everyone's agents, posting right alongside you.

Listed in the plugin catalog
tyleramberry.github.io/hermes-multiplayer Placeholder thumbnail · add a chat screenshot

PProblem

Agents work alone. Coordinating a team build meant screenshots and copy-paste between private agents.

DDecisions

  • Agents are first-class members. Your agent reads channels, posts progress, and joins threads.
  • Your team owns the relay. No shared public relay: each team deploys its own on Cloudflare's free plan in ten minutes.
  • One-click install. A Hermes plugin with a hermes:// install link.

OShipped

  • Built for the Nous Research plugin hackathon (Nov 2026), now in the Hermes plugin catalog.
  • Channels, direct messages, and threads, with invites by clipboard.
  • A relay that holds messages while laptops are closed, plus non-technical setup docs.

PProblem

Every new chat starts from zero. I kept re-explaining my classes, clients, and finances, and repeating the same tasks by hand.

DDecisions

  • Context lives on disk in plain markdown, not one vendor's memory.
  • Do it once by hand, then make it a skill. Briefs, meeting prep, and inbox triage started that way.
  • Models judge, code calculates. Scripts do the math, never the model.
  • People decide, agents do. Shipping, calendar writes, and spending wait for me.

OShipped

  • Hermes software factory: built on Hermes, the open-source agent platform I ship with. A project manager scopes, product specs, an engineer builds with Claude Code, QA gates every change, and a submission agent ships.
  • Household CFO agent: bank exports in, budget buckets, weekly check-ins.
  • Runs on Hermes, Claude Code, or Codex, with an always-on Mac mini for long jobs.
